## Features

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### Summary
Provisional IRS deductions for the year, sector by sector, with your yearly limits and how much you can still deduct, calculated from your tax situation.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### Invoices
Every invoice issued to your NIF, filterable by sector. Classify an invoice in one tap and apply the same sector to every other invoice from that seller.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### Pending
Invoices still waiting for a classification, in one list. Select several and classify them together.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### Register Invoices
Add an invoice the seller never reported by scanning its QR code, or enter it by hand with one line per VAT rate.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### Expenses
Where the money went: spending per sector in a chart, and the ten sellers you spend the most with.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### Profile
Several accounts on one device, your tax situation (household, dependents, income, housing) for exact limits, and the year's invoices exported as a CSV for Numbers or Excel.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## iPhone and Mac

- **iPhone:** five tabs (Resumo, Faturas, Pendentes, Despesas, Perfil), standard Apple controls throughout.
- **Mac:** a sidebar, the invoice list with the selected invoice beside it, and the summary as a grid of sector cards.

Both apps share the same code, so every feature works the same on both.

## How It Works

- The app signs in to the official e-Fatura website straight from your device, with your own NIF and password, and reads the same pages the website shows you.
- Your login stays in the device's Keychain. Nothing passes through a server of ours, because there is none.
- The interface is in Portuguese, like the portal.
